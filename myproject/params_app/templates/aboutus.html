Our Mission

Reducing the daily friction of finding a parking spot

We started PARMS because we understood the frustration of circling blocks looking for parking. Our platform gives drivers instant visibility into available spaces, lets them reserve a spot before they arrive, and handles payment automatically — so they can focus on where they're going, not where they'll park.

For parking operators, PARMS provides real-time occupancy data, automated billing, and management tools that replace manual processes — increasing efficiency and revenue without adding overhead.
